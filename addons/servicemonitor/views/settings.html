Browser notifications

A notification from the browser when a monitor goes down and when it is back up. Each person turns them on for their own browser with the bell on the page; browsers allow them only on HTTPS (or localhost).

Turned off, the bell is hidden and no notifications are sent from that page. While notifications are on, the page keeps checking in a background tab. Monitors under Don't notify are skipped.

Slack notifications

Posts to a Slack channel when monitors go down and when they are back up, through an incoming webhook (Slack › Apps › Incoming Webhooks › Add to Slack, then pick the channel).

Don't notify

Groups and monitors left out of Slack and browser notifications. They are still checked and still show on the pages and in Incidents. A group covers all of its monitors, also ones added to it later.
